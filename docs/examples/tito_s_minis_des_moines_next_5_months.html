Tito's minis, Des Moines, next 5 months

Asked: “Monthly forecast of Tito's minis in Des Moines for the next 5 months”

Tito's minis in Des Moines are forecast at about 30,900 bottles over the next five months, up 7% from the same period last year (28,800). The biggest year-over-year gains are expected in November and January, both up 18%, while September and October look roughly flat. This forecast is built on a ridge model that has been backtested against a simple seasonal-naive baseline and comes out about 8% more accurate overall, though error rates are still fairly high (roughly 37-39% depending on how far ahead you look), so treat the totals as directional rather than precise.

Assumptions the agent made:

  • "Tito's minis" means the 50ml bottle size, including both the single mini item and the 4-pack-of-minis item.
  • "Des Moines" refers to the city of Des Moines specifically, not West Des Moines or all of Polk County.
  • Target metric is bottles sold (not dollars or liters) since not otherwise specified.
  • Forecasting one combined total series (not split by product or sub-area) for the next 5 months.

Tito's Handmade Vodka Minis (50ml, incl. 4-pack) in Des Moines: monthly bottles, Sep 2026 to Jan 2027. Model: the average of the top 3 of 96 configurations, blended 50% with 50% same month last year (explained below), 8% more accurate than repeating last year over a 20-origin backtest on Sep 2024 to Aug 2026, months the model never trained on.

How this forecast is made. 96 model configurations were scored on Sep 2022 to Aug 2024, before the test window; a tuning error below 1 beats repeating the same month last year. The forecast uses the average of the top 3:

  1. Ridge regression predicting monthly bottles directly from the values 1, 2, 3 and 12 months back, regularization strength 10, trained on this series only (all history). Tuning error 0.990 vs the baseline.
  2. LightGBM (gradient-boosted trees, 7 leaves) predicting monthly bottles directly from the last 12 months, trained on this series plus the same product in the 15 busiest counties (all history). Tuning error 1.000 vs the baseline.
  3. Ridge regression predicting monthly bottles directly from the values 1, 2, 3 and 12 months back, regularization strength 1, trained on this series only (all history). Tuning error 1.034 vs the baseline.

Each month's forecast is then 50% this model average plus 50% of what sold in the same month last year. That mix scored 0.871 on the tuning window, the best of every combination tried (1 or 3 models, 0% to 100% model), vs 0.895 for the best model alone and 0.928 for the 3-model average alone. The pieces make different mistakes, so the mix partly cancels them.

Aggregation SQL (generated by the harness, run under the read-only role):

SELECT date_trunc('month', l.ordered_on)::date AS month,
       'total' AS series,
       l.store_no,
       sum(l.sales_bottles) AS value
FROM sales.invoice_line l
JOIN sales.item i USING (item_no)
JOIN sales.store s USING (store_no)
WHERE l.item_no = ANY(ARRAY['38185', '38194', '38180', '938180'])
  AND s.city = ANY(ARRAY['DES MOINES'])
  AND l.ordered_on >= '2016-01-01'
GROUP BY 1, 2, 3
ORDER BY 1, 2, 3
