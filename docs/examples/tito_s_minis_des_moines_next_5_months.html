Tito's minis, Des Moines, next 5 months

Asked: “Monthly forecast of Tito's minis in Des Moines for the next 5 months”

Tito's Minis (50ml) in Des Moines are forecast at 3,410 bottles over the next five months, up 10% versus the same months last year (3,090 bottles). Growth is led by November and January, each up roughly 24-25% year over year, while September and October look close to flat. The model is 11% more accurate than a simple seasonal-naive baseline in backtesting, though errors still run around 35% by month, so treat this as a directional guide rather than a precise number. No series were skipped or left unvalidated in this forecast.

Assumptions the agent made:

  • "Tito's minis" means Tito's Handmade Vodka in 50ml bottles, including both the single mini (item 38194) and the 4-pack mini (item 38185).
  • "Des Moines" is treated as the city of Des Moines (not West Des Moines or all of Polk County).
  • Forecast target is bottles sold (sales_bottles), since no dollar or volume metric was specified.
  • Horizon is 5 months ahead as requested, with one combined total series (not broken out by item).

Aggregation SQL (generated by the harness, run under the read-only role):

SELECT date_trunc('month', l.ordered_on)::date AS month,
       'total' AS series,
       sum(l.sales_bottles) AS value
FROM sales.invoice_line l
JOIN sales.item i USING (item_no)
JOIN sales.store s USING (store_no)
WHERE l.item_no = ANY(ARRAY['38185', '38194', '38180', '938180'])
  AND s.city = ANY(ARRAY['DES MOINES'])
  AND l.ordered_on >= '2016-01-01'
GROUP BY 1, 2
ORDER BY 1, 2
