How the model was chosen

Model: the average of the top 3 of 96 configurations (explained below); 11% less accurate than repeating last year over a 13-origin backtest on Sep 2024 to Aug 2026, months the model never trained on.

How this forecast is made. 96 model configurations were scored on Sep 2022 to Aug 2024, before the test window; a selection error below 1 beats repeating the same month last year. The forecast uses the average of the top 3:

  1. Ridge regression predicting monthly liters directly from the values 1, 2, 3 and 12 months back, regularization strength 10, trained on this series plus the same product in the 15 busiest counties (the last 5 years). Selection error 0.683 vs the baseline.
  2. Ridge regression predicting monthly liters directly from the last 12 months, regularization strength 10, trained on this series plus the same product in the 15 busiest counties (the last 5 years). Selection error 0.685 vs the baseline.
  3. Ridge regression predicting monthly liters directly from the values 1, 2, 3 and 12 months back, regularization strength 1, trained on this series plus the same product in the 15 busiest counties (the last 5 years). Selection error 0.685 vs the baseline.

The models alone scored better than any mix with last year's numbers, so the forecast is the average of the top 3 with no blending (selection error 0.603).

SELECT date_trunc('month', l.ordered_on)::date AS month,
       'total' AS series,
       l.store_no,
       sum(l.sales_liters) AS value
FROM sales.invoice_line l
JOIN sales.item i USING (item_no)
JOIN sales.store s USING (store_no)
WHERE TRUE
  AND TRUE
  AND l.ordered_on >= '2016-01-01'
