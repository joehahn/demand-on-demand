Statewide total liters, next 12 months

Asked: “Total liters of liquor sold statewide over the next 12 months”

Statewide volume over the next 12 months is forecast at about 21.1 million liters, down 2% from the same months last year's 21.5 million liters. Monthly changes range from small gains like +2% in March and August to declines of -6% in October and July. In backtesting, the model was 4% less accurate than a simple seasonal-naive baseline overall, performing on par or better through the first six months ahead but noticeably worse than the baseline from 9 to 12 months out, so nearer-term months should be trusted more than the tail end of the forecast. No series were flagged as unvalidated or skipped.

Assumptions the agent made:

  • "Liquor" interpreted as all products across all categories (no filtering by spirit type).
  • Statewide means all of Iowa combined into a single total series.
  • Horizon set to 12 months as requested.
  • Target metric is sales_liters (total liters sold) since the user asked for liters.

All products in Iowa statewide: monthly liters, Sep 2026 to Aug 2027. Model: the average of the top 3 of 96 configurations (explained below), about as accurate as repeating last year over a 13-origin backtest on Sep 2024 to Aug 2026, months the model never trained on.

How this forecast is made. 96 model configurations were scored on Sep 2022 to Aug 2024, before the test window; a tuning error below 1 beats repeating the same month last year. The forecast uses the average of the top 3:

  1. LightGBM (gradient-boosted trees, 7 leaves) predicting monthly liters directly from the values 1, 2, 3 and 12 months back, trained on this series plus the same product in the 15 busiest counties (all history). Tuning error 0.614 vs the baseline.
  2. Ridge regression predicting the change from the same month last year from the values 1, 2, 3 and 12 months back, regularization strength 1, trained on this series only (the last 5 years). Tuning error 0.654 vs the baseline.
  3. Ridge regression predicting monthly liters directly from the last 12 months, regularization strength 10, trained on this series plus the same product in the 15 busiest counties (the last 5 years). Tuning error 0.654 vs the baseline.

The models alone scored better than any mix with last year's numbers, so the forecast is the average of the top 3 with no blending (tuning error 0.600).

The data

Monthly liters from 2016-01 through Aug 2026, read from the clean warehouse: duplicate export rows and zero lines removed, renumbered products joined, categories in today's taxonomy, one spelling per city. See what was fixed. A series starts at its first sale.

Aggregation SQL (generated by the harness, run under the read-only role):

SELECT date_trunc('month', l.ordered_on)::date AS month,
       'total' AS series,
       sum(l.sales_liters) AS value
FROM sales.invoice_line l
JOIN sales.item i USING (item_no)
JOIN sales.store s USING (store_no)
WHERE TRUE
  AND TRUE
  AND l.ordered_on >= '2016-01-01'
GROUP BY 1, 2
ORDER BY 1, 2
