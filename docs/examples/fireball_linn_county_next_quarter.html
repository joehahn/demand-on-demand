Fireball, Linn County, next quarter

Asked: “What revenue should we expect from Fireball in Linn County next quarter?”

Fireball sales in Linn County are forecast at about $553,000 over the next quarter, down 4% from the same months last year ($578,000). The dip is led by September, projected down 11%, while November is expected to run 4% above last year. Backtesting shows the model is 9% more accurate than a simple seasonal-naive baseline across all three forecast months, so it can be reasonably trusted for near-term planning. No series were skipped or left unvalidated.

Assumptions the agent made:

  • Fireball interpreted as all Fireball Cinnamon Whiskey products and flavor variants (all bottle sizes and special packaging) sold by Sazerac, not just the core 750ml SKU.
  • Revenue means sales_dollars.
  • Next quarter interpreted as the next 3 months.
  • Linn County means the Iowa county (FIPS 19113), not just the city of Cedar Rapids.
  • Forecast is a single total series for the county, not broken out by product or city.

Aggregation SQL (generated by the harness, run under the read-only role):

SELECT date_trunc('month', l.ordered_on)::date AS month,
       'total' AS series,
       sum(l.sales_dollars) AS value
FROM sales.invoice_line l
JOIN sales.item i USING (item_no)
JOIN sales.store s USING (store_no)
WHERE l.item_no = ANY(ARRAY['100015', '100413', '100423', '100535', '100642', '100847', '100938', '101151', '101200', '101512', '101513', '101515', '101710', '101988', '102031', '102057', '102096', '102214', '102445', '102556', '102780', '102945', '103039', '103152', '103153', '103155', '103156', '64858', '64863', '64864', '...'])
  AND s.county_fips = ANY(ARRAY['19113'])
  AND l.ordered_on >= '2016-01-01'
GROUP BY 1, 2
ORDER BY 1, 2
