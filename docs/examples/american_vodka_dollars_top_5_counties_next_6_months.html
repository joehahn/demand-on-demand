How the model was chosen

Model: the average of the top 3 of 96 configurations (explained below); about as accurate as repeating last year over a 19-origin backtest on Sep 2024 to Aug 2026, months the model never trained on.

How this forecast is made. 96 model configurations were scored on Sep 2022 to Aug 2024, before the test window; a selection error below 1 beats repeating the same month last year. The forecast uses the average of the top 3:

  1. Ridge regression predicting the change from the same month last year from the values 1, 2, 3 and 12 months back, regularization strength 1, trained on these 5 series only (the last 5 years). Selection error 0.745 vs the baseline.
  2. Ridge regression predicting the change from the same month last year from the values 1, 2, 3 and 12 months back, regularization strength 1, trained on these 5 series only (all history). Selection error 0.761 vs the baseline.
  3. Ridge regression predicting the change from the same month last year from the values 1, 2, 3 and 12 months back, regularization strength 10, trained on these 5 series plus the same product in the 15 busiest other counties (all history). Selection error 0.772 vs the baseline.

The models alone scored better than any mix with last year's numbers, so the forecast is the average of the top 3 with no blending (selection error 0.736).
