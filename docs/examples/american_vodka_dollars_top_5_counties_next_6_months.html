American vodka dollars, top 5 counties, next 6 months

Asked: “American vodka sales dollars for each of the five largest counties, next 6 months”

American vodka sales across the top 5 counties are forecast at about $15.3 million over the next six months, roughly flat (+1%) versus the same months last year's $15.2 million. Monthly results are mixed, with November standing out at +10% versus last year while September and January dip slightly (-2%). Backtesting shows this model is about 4% less accurate than a simple seasonal-naive baseline, and it tends to underperform that baseline at the 4- and 6-month horizons, so treat the later months with some caution. Polk county dominates the forecast at roughly $6.86 million of the total.

Assumptions the agent made:

  • American vodka means the AMERICAN VODKAS category only, not the separate AMERICAN FLAVORED VODKA category.
  • Five largest counties determined by latest available county population estimate (Polk, Linn, Scott, Johnson, Black Hawk).
  • Forecast target is sales_dollars as requested (revenue), forecasted separately for each of the 5 counties.
  • Horizon set to 6 months as requested.

American Vodkas in Polk, Linn, Scott, Johnson, Black Hawk counties: monthly dollars, Sep 2026 to Feb 2027. Model: the average of the top 3 of 96 configurations (explained below), about as accurate as repeating last year over a 19-origin backtest on Sep 2024 to Aug 2026, months the model never trained on.

How this forecast is made. 96 model configurations were scored on Sep 2022 to Aug 2024, before the test window; a tuning error below 1 beats repeating the same month last year. The forecast uses the average of the top 3:

  1. Ridge regression predicting the change from the same month last year from the values 1, 2, 3 and 12 months back, regularization strength 1, trained on these 5 series only (the last 5 years). Tuning error 0.745 vs the baseline.
  2. Ridge regression predicting the change from the same month last year from the values 1, 2, 3 and 12 months back, regularization strength 1, trained on these 5 series only (all history). Tuning error 0.761 vs the baseline.
  3. Ridge regression predicting the change from the same month last year from the values 1, 2, 3 and 12 months back, regularization strength 10, trained on these 5 series plus the same product in the 15 busiest other counties (all history). Tuning error 0.772 vs the baseline.

The models alone scored better than any mix with last year's numbers, so the forecast is the average of the top 3 with no blending (tuning error 0.736).

Aggregation SQL (generated by the harness, run under the read-only role):

SELECT date_trunc('month', l.ordered_on)::date AS month,
       coalesce(s.county_name, '(no county)') AS series,
       sum(l.sales_dollars) AS value
FROM sales.invoice_line l
JOIN sales.item i USING (item_no)
JOIN sales.store s USING (store_no)
WHERE l.item_no = ANY(ARRAY['36122', '37091', '38058', '38071', '36535', '40196', '78293', '976811', '638071', '101873', '102149', '102747', '102754', '103157', '937101', '937117', '33221', '33642', '33647', '37988', '34364', '34546', '34578', '34579', '34698', '34819', '34820', '34821', '35313', '35314', '...'])
  AND s.county_fips = ANY(ARRAY['19153', '19113', '19163', '19103', '19013'])
  AND l.ordered_on >= '2016-01-01'
GROUP BY 1, 2
ORDER BY 1, 2
